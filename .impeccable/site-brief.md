# Surface brief: the Weftday website (site/index.html)

Scope: the public home page. Mode: Persuade. Audience: people whose day is meetings, deciding whether to open the app; and people checking it can be trusted. Action: open the app (nothing to install) or try it with sample data. Proof: the real app, shown in pictures and one clip made from its sample data; the code on GitHub; the plain statement of where data lives. Constraints: no invented users, numbers or logos; plain words; Teams must be mentioned; a quiet "by CRCKD".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The day is one woven thread, kept in a card file that is yours. The page is that card file: a drawer of index cards with coloured tabs. It refuses the category's default of a centred headline over a product shot and a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: A deep spruce-green box as the page ground, with a faint cloth weave. White index cards with rounded top corners and a coloured tab on the top edge of each: coral for Record, green for Notes, blue for Calendar, violet for People, amber for Yours. Type: Bricolage Grotesque for headings, heavy and tight; Figtree for reading. Buttons are plain pills. No gradients, no glass.

STORY: The visitor understands that calendar, recorder and notes are one thing here; believes it is private because there is no server and the code is open; opens the app or the sample.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The green box. Top bar with the mark, the tabs as navigation, and "Open Weftday". The headline "Your day, woven together." large at the left, a two-sentence line under it, two buttons. Rising from the bottom edge, the first white card with its five coloured tabs, holding the clip of the real app.

FORM: a card file drawer; the assigned position on my ordered list was 7 of 7. seed: none (unavailable to record; the roll was run in an unattended session and its key is not kept here).

FINISH: every section is a card in the drawer; the tabs are the navigation; checked at phone and desktop widths.
