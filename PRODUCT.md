# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript modules. No server, no build step, no dependencies. The app is a static folder that runs entirely in the browser (installable as a PWA). The website is a second static folder. Both are hosted free on static hosting; anyone can self-host their own copy.

## Users

- People whose working day is meetings and follow-ups: founders, operators, consultants, sales and partnership people. Usually several Google accounts (work, personal, a client's). They open Weftday at the start of the day at a desk, keep it open in a browser tab through meetings, and come back to it between calls. Not technical.
- People checking whether to trust it: someone deciding to connect their Google account wants to see that it is open source, that nothing leaves their device, and who is behind it.
- Developers who may self-host or contribute.

## Product Purpose

Weftday is one calm place for the day: every Google calendar and task together, the people in each meeting, a recorder for meetings and the screen, and notes that write themselves from the recording. It exists so that a person walks into a meeting prepared and walks out with the notes and next steps already written, without paying for five separate tools or handing their data to a server.

## Positioning

A meeting notebook, recorder and planner in one, free and open source, that runs in the person's own browser. Granola and Loom are separate paid products that keep recordings on their servers; Cap is an open-source recorder without the calendar or notes. Weftday's difference is the weave: the meeting on the calendar, the recording of it, the notes from it and the tasks that follow are one thread, and all of it stays on the person's device or in their own Google account.

Weftday is a product of CRCKD. More CRCKD products will follow, so Weftday is a member of a family, not a copy of the CRCKD site.

## Operating Context

- Recording uses the browser's own screen and microphone capture. Notes use an AI key the person gets free from Google and types in themselves.
- Recordings stay in the browser. A share link uploads one recording to the person's own Google Drive when they ask.
- The app is at an app address; the website is separate and links to it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No accounts of Weftday's own, no server, no analytics, no paid tiers. Nothing may depend on a paid service.
- Must work on a phone, with the keyboard alone, in light and dark, and with reduced motion.
- Words on screen are plain. One small step per screen. Never a wall of options. The owner has said the app must not feel overwhelming, and that an intro tour of ten steps is too long.
- The owner finds the current look dull and wants it to feel warm and inviting (Granola), fresh (Loom), and as finished as Cap's site and repository.
- Google sign-in is still in testing mode with Google; an approved Google app and a Mac app come later.
- No testimonials, customer logos, user counts or press exist. None may be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Weftday. A weft is the thread woven across a loom; the name is the day woven together.
- Belongs to the CRCKD family (maroon #3E0A18, cream #F4EEE3, gold #D7A444; Archivo and DM Sans on crckd.live). The owner does not want a replica of crckd.live: Weftday has its own look, with the family tie kept to a quiet "by CRCKD" line.
- Voice: plain, specific, kind. Describes what happens. No jargon, no hype, no em dashes.

## Evidence on Hand

- The working app with realistic sample data (`?demo`), from which real screenshots and screen recordings can be made.
- Facts that may be stated: free; open source; runs in the browser with no server; recordings stay on the device; notes written by AI from the recording with speakers and times; share links through the person's own Google Drive; several Google accounts in one calendar; works offline once opened; self-hostable.

## Product Principles

1. The day is one thread: meeting, recording, notes and tasks belong together.
2. Calm first. One step per screen; the tool disappears into the task.
3. Yours stays yours: on your device or in your own Google account, and the code is open to prove it.
4. Free to run, for everyone, forever. Paid AI is optional and the person's own choice.
5. Say only what is true and can be shown.
