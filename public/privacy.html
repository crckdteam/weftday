Planner privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Planner is a personal planning app that runs entirely in your web browser. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Planner has no server of its own. Your calendar events, tasks and sign-in details stay in your browser and in your own Google account. The people who made Planner cannot see them, and they are not sold, shared or used for advertising.

What Planner accesses

  • Google Calendar. When you connect a Google account, Planner asks for access to your calendars. It uses this to show your events, to create, change or delete events when you ask it to, to send your replies to invitations, and to keep a calendar called “My Planner” that holds your tasks.
  • Your email address. Planner reads the address of each account you connect so it can label that account on screen.

Where your information is kept

  • In your browser. Your tasks, settings, a short-lived copy of this week's events, and the keys that let Planner reach your Google account are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device.
  • In your Google account. If you choose it, your tasks are also saved as events in the “My Planner” calendar in your own Google account.

Your browser sends requests straight to Google. Nothing passes through, or is stored on, any server run by Planner.

What Planner does not do

  • It does not send your calendar data or tasks to anyone other than Google.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not use your Google data to train AI models.

Planner's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Hosting

The app's files are delivered by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when your browser downloads those files. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Removing your information

  • To disconnect an account, use “Remove” next to it in Planner, or remove Planner at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • To erase what is stored on a device, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • To remove your saved tasks from Google, delete the “My Planner” calendar in Google Calendar.
