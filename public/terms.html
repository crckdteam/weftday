The short version

Weftday is free, and it runs in your own browser. Your information stays with you, so looking after it is up to you. Weftday is offered as it is, with no promises that it will always work or never lose anything. Use it lawfully, and tell people before you record them.

What Weftday is

Weftday shows your Google calendars and tasks in one place, keeps your meeting notes, records meetings and your screen, and can write notes with the help of AI services you choose. It has no server of its own: it runs in your browser and talks directly to the services you connect.

Your information is yours

  • Everything you put into Weftday stays in your browser and in your own Google account. The people who make Weftday cannot see it, recover it or delete it for you.
  • That means keeping it safe is up to you. Recordings in particular live only in the browser you made them in. Download the ones you want to keep; clearing the browser’s data removes them.
  • How information is handled is described in the privacy policy.

Accounts and services you connect

  • You connect your own Google account, and you can remove Weftday from it at any time. Your use of Google is covered by Google’s own terms.
  • AI and web-search features use a key that you get from that service and type in yourself. That service’s terms and prices apply to you, and anything it charges is between you and it. Weftday never charges you.
  • Keep your keys, and any link or code Weftday gives you for setting up another device, to yourself.

Recording

  • Tell everyone in a meeting before you record it, and get their agreement where the law asks for it. The rules differ from place to place, and following them is your responsibility.
  • Only record and share things you have the right to.
  • A share link opens for anyone who has it. Send it only to people who should see the recording, and switch the link off when it is no longer needed.

Notes written by AI

AI can mishear, leave things out, or state something that was never said. Read what it writes before you rely on it or pass it on. Weftday’s AI features are not legal, medical or financial advice.
