Weftday

All your calendars and tasks in one place.

Weftday is a small personal app. It shows the calendars from several Google accounts side by side and lets you plan your day by placing tasks as time blocks next to your meetings.

Open Weftday

What it does

  • Shows events from all your connected Google calendars in day, week, month and list views.
  • Lets you add tasks, give them a time, and tick them off.
  • Lets you create, edit and reply to calendar events.
  • Can save your tasks in a calendar called “Weftday” in your own Google account, so your devices stay in step and your phone can remind you.
  • Can keep your connected accounts the same on all your devices, so a new phone or computer needs no Google login.
  • Helps you prepare for meetings: private notes on any meeting, how often you have met each person, and your earlier notes with them.

How it uses your Google account

Weftday asks for access to Google Calendar so it can show your events, save the changes you make, and keep the “Weftday” calendar. It runs entirely in your own browser and talks to Google directly. There is no Weftday server, and nobody else receives your calendar data. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Weftday is a personal project and is not made by or affiliated with Google.
